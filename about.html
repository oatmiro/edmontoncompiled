Edmonton's tech & design events, all in one place

Edmonton Compiled is home to upcoming events in Edmonton's tech and design world. This project aims to be a comprehensive source of all tech events in the city, so that you no longer hear the all too common "I didn't know about that until after it happened!" The homepage aims to show upcoming shows while the newsletter delivers this week's events striaght to your inbox on monday, so you can add something of value to your week.

Have a tech or design event you want to see listed here? Get in contact and submit your event to be included on the homepage and the weekly newsletter.
