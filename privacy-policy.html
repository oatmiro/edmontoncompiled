Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Edmonton Compiled ("we," "us," or "our") operates edmontoncompiled.ca. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we collect and use your information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you subscribe to the Edmonton Compiled newsletter, we collect your email address.

Our newsletter is managed and delivered using MailerLite, a third-party email marketing service. In connection with your newsletter subscription, MailerLite may also process information such as:

  • Your email address
  • Your subscription date and time
  • Your IP address, where collected
  • Information about whether you open our emails
  • Information about links you click in our emails
  • Your unsubscribe status and related subscription activity

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, payment information, mailing addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information through our newsletter signup.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send the Edmonton Compiled newsletter.
  • Provide information, updates, events, and other content related to Edmonton Compiled.
  • Manage newsletter subscriptions and unsubscribes.
  • Understand basic newsletter engagement, such as whether emails are opened or links are clicked, so that we can improve our communications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address.

3. MailerLite

We use MailerLite to collect and manage newsletter subscriptions and to send our newsletter.

When you subscribe to our newsletter, your information is transferred to and processed by MailerLite in accordance with its own privacy practices.

MailerLite may use technologies such as cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies in emails to provide information about email delivery and engagement, including whether an email is opened or a link is clicked.

For more information about how MailerLite handles personal information, please review MailerLite's Privacy Policy.

4. Newsletter Communications

By subscribing to the Edmonton Compiled newsletter, you consent to receiving newsletter communications from us.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in any Edmonton Compiled newsletter or by contacting us at edmontoncompiled@gmail.com.

After unsubscribing, you will no longer receive our newsletter, although certain information may remain in MailerLite's systems as necessary for record-keeping, legal, security, or suppression purposes.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your email address and related newsletter information may be processed by MailerLite as our third-party service provider for the purpose of managing subscriptions and delivering our newsletter.

We may also disclose information where required to do so by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, website, users, or services.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website or newsletter may use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies.

In particular, MailerLite may use tracking technologies in newsletters to provide us with information about email delivery and engagement, such as whether a newsletter has been opened or whether links within it have been clicked.

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in our emails.

Your web browser may also allow you to control or block certain cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain your email address and related newsletter information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our newsletter services and maintain appropriate records.

If you unsubscribe, your information may be removed from our active mailing list. Some information may be retained by us or our service providers where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you.

You may contact us to:

  • Ask what personal information we have about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request removal of your email address from our newsletter mailing list.
  • Withdraw your consent to receive our newsletter.
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected or used.

To make a privacy-related request, contact:

Edmonton Compiled
Email: edmontoncompiled@gmail.com
Website: edmontoncompiled.ca

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Edmonton Compiled does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating profiles or accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided us with an email address without appropriate consent, please contact us at edmontoncompiled@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, newsletter, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us:

Edmonton Compiled
Email: edmontoncompiled@gmail.com
Website: edmontoncompiled.ca
